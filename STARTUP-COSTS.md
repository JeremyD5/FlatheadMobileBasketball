# Startup Costs

Mobile arcade basketball trailer, Flathead Valley MT. Concept modeled on [Hoop Dallas](https://www.hoopdallas.com/) / [Hoop Trailer](https://hooptrailer.com/).

**Status:** exploratory. Nothing committed. Numbers are estimates as of September 2026 — verify every price before relying on it.

**Baseline for comparison:** the licensed Hoop Trailer package is **$60,000** plus **$250/month** for hosting, Google Business setup, cards, wristbands, and a 100-mile exclusive territory. Everything below is the DIY alternative. See [pricing / licensing page](https://www.hooptrailer.com/pricing-mobile-arcade-basketball-trailer-rental-licensing-opportunity).

## 1. Trailer — sourced used, locally

Target spec: **8.5 ft wide × 16 ft long, tandem axle, 7,000 lb GVWR, electric brakes, 7 ft+ interior height.**

Buying used locally instead of new saves roughly $4,000–6,000 and eliminates lead time. New factory-direct comparison point: [MakeMyTrailer 8.5x16 tandem axle](https://makemytrailer.com/8-5x16-tandem-axle-enclosed-trailer-with-awning/) at ~$9,026 with Lippert 3,500 lb axles and electric brakes.

### Where to look

- [Facebook Marketplace — vehicles/trailers](https://www.facebook.com/marketplace/category/trailers) — set radius to 250 mi from Kalispell to pull in Missoula, Spokane, Coeur d'Alene, Great Falls
- [Craigslist Western Montana](https://missoula.craigslist.org/search/sss?query=enclosed+trailer)
- [Craigslist Kalispell/Flathead](https://kalispell.craigslist.org/search/sss?query=enclosed+trailer)
- Search terms: `enclosed trailer`, `cargo trailer`, `8.5x16`, `tandem axle`, `V-nose`, `race trailer`, `concession trailer`

### Expected pricing

| Condition | Price |
|---|---|
| Rough but structurally sound (needs floor, lights, tires) | $3,500–5,500 |
| Solid used, 5–10 yrs old, good floor and axles | $5,500–8,000 |
| Late-model, low use, already has some upgrades | $8,000–11,000 |

### Inspection checklist before you buy

- [ ] **Interior height** — measure it. A 6'6" ceiling is a dealbreaker; you need 7 ft minimum, ideally 7'6". This is the #1 thing that kills an otherwise good trailer.
- [ ] **Floor** — walk every square foot. Soft spots mean plywood replacement, ~$600–1,200.
- [ ] **Axles and brakes** — 3,500 lb axles minimum, tandem, with functioning electric brakes. Test them.
- [ ] **Tires** — check DOT date codes. Anything over 6 years old needs replacing regardless of tread, ~$500–900 for four plus spare.
- [ ] **Frame rust** — look at the tongue, the crossmembers, and the bottom of the side walls.
- [ ] **Title in hand** — no title, no deal. Montana will not register it.
- [ ] **Roof** — check for soft spots and seam sealant condition from inside on a bright day.
- [ ] **Wall studs** — 16" on center is fine, 24" is common on cheap trailers and matters when you cut in big doors.

### A note on cutting doors

The whole business model depends on one long side opening up. Cutting a ~14 ft opening into a used trailer's sidewall means you are removing structure. Do not do this without someone who understands trailer framing. Budget for header reinforcement.

### Insurance — where to quote

This is the line most likely to surprise you. Bounce house and inflatable operators are the closest analog underwriters have; expect to be classed similarly.

- [Cossio Insurance Agency](https://www.cossioinsurance.com/insurance/inflatable-insurance.html) — the most commonly used specialist in this niche
- [Bounce House Insurance (instant online rates)](https://www.bounce-house-insurance.com/)
- [XINSURANCE](https://www.xinsurance.com/risk-class/inflatables-insurance-bounce-house-insurance/)
- [Kelly Insurance Group](https://kellyinsurancegroup.com/inflatables-and-bounce-house-insurance/)
- [Prime Insurance](https://www.primeis.com/product-lines/commercial-liability/inflatables/)
- [MoneyGeek comparison overview](https://www.moneygeek.com/insurance/business/bounce-house/)

Reported ranges: home-based rental operators typically land $500–1,500/yr; multi-unit commercial operators $1,500–4,000/yr. One source reports rates starting around $107/month. Your risk profile is arguably lower than an inflatable operator (no wind-lift exposure, no unanchored structure), which is worth arguing during underwriting.

**Get a real quote before committing to anything else.** If this comes back at $4,000/yr it materially changes the business case.

## 10. Payback

Based on Flathead Valley market sizing (115,429 county residents, ~24,400 under 18, per [Flathead County](https://flatheadcounty.gov/about-flathead-county)):

| Scenario | Bookings | Avg ticket | Season revenue | Net after opex |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 28 | $525 | ~$14,700 | ~$7,000 |
| Base (year 1) | 45 | $600 | ~$27,000 | ~$19,000 |
| Strong (year 2–3) | 70 | $650 | ~$45,500 | ~$33,000 |

Against a ~$30,000 DIY build: **payback in roughly 1.5–2 seasons.** Against the $60,000 licensed package: 2–3 seasons.

Adding a shore-power inlet and a heater to work indoors November–April could add 10–15 bookings and $5,000–7,500, which is the single biggest lever on this whole model.

### Pricing comparables from the Hoop network

| Market | Rate |
|---|---|
| [Idaho Falls](https://www.hoopidahofalls.com/) (closest small-market comp) | $400 / 4 hr, +$100/hr |
| [Central PA](https://www.hoopcentralpa.com/) | $400 / 4 hr, +$100/hr, $2/mi beyond 20 mi |
| [San Diego](https://hoopsandiego.com/) | $600 / 4 hr, +$150/hr, $2/mi beyond 10 mi |
| [Orlando](https://www.hooporlando.com/) | $500 / 2 hr or $750 / 4 hr, $3/mi |

**Proposed Flathead pricing:** $375 / 2 hr · $550 / 4 hr · +$125/hr · $900–1,400 full day · free within 20 mi of Kalispell, $2.50/mi beyond.

## 11. Local market notes

**Competition** is inflatables only — no arcade trailer in the valley:
- [Inflate406](https://www.inflate406.com/) (Kalispell)
- [Monkey Business Adventures](https://monkeybusinessadventures.org/) (Kalispell/Whitefish/Columbia Falls)
- [Celebrate Event & Party Rental](https://www.celebraterentals.com/) (Bigfork, serves the whole valley)

**Target bookings beyond birthday parties** — these are what make or break the season:
- Glacier Range Riders home games, late May through August
- [Northwest Montana Fair & Rodeo](https://www.nwmtfair.com/), mid-August, 100+ commercial exhibitors
- [Huckleberry Days](https://glaciermt.com/montana-event/52272), Whitefish Depot Park, early August
- [Under the Big Sky](https://www.underthebigskyfest.com/), Big Mountain Ranch, mid-July
- Whitefish Farmers Market (Tuesdays, June–September) and Whitefish Summer Concert Series
- School districts: Kalispell, Whitefish, Columbia Falls — fall carnivals and field days
- Corporate: Logan Health (largest valley employer), Glacier Bank, Nomad Global Communication Solutions
- Whitefish and Bigfork weddings — kid entertainment at receptions

Seasonal event calendar: [Flathead Beacon summer events](https://flatheadbeacon.com/2026/05/23/cant-miss-events-for-summer-2026/)
