# Buying the Divider Panels Off the Shelf (US)

The Chinese machines ship a welded steel frame with mesh infill. The US equivalent is a commodity product sold by an entire industry: **wire partition and machine guarding panels.**

That's the search term. Not "arcade divider," not "custom cage" — **wire mesh partition panel** or **machine guard panel**.

---

## Why this works

Decomposing the divider into a **rectangle + triangle** is the right move, because those two pieces have completely different sourcing:

| Piece | What it is | How to get it |
|---|---|---|
| **Rectangle** (~70% of the panel) | Stock framed wire mesh panel | **Buy off the shelf.** Sold in dozens of stock sizes, ships in a day |
| **Triangle** (~30%) | "Tapered" or "stairway" panel | Custom order from the same maker, **or** local fab |

The industry already solves the triangle. Guarding a staircase, a conveyor incline, or a press brake means angled panels, so **"tapered panels"** and **"stairway panels"** are catalog terms, not exotic requests.

## Spec to ask for

Matching the reference photo:

| Spec | Ask for |
|---|---|
| Mesh | **2"×2" welded, 10 gauge** (WireCrafters RapidWireHD) or **2"×1" woven, 10 gauge** (Style 840 — the automotive machine-guard standard) |
| Frame | 1-1/4" × 1-1/4" × 1/8" hot rolled angle, or MaxGuard channel for a cleaner face |
| Finish | **Black powder coat** — most stock is gray or safety yellow, so specify |
| Rectangle | ~48" H × 93" W (call it 4' × 8' and adapt) |
| Triangle | Right triangle, 93" base × 24" rise, same mesh and frame |

Ten pieces total for five dividers: five rectangles, five triangles.

---

## Rough pricing

Distributor retail on stock panels runs roughly **$120 for a 27"×60"** up to **$400+ for a 2'×8'**, though marketplace listings inflate this. **Direct from the manufacturer on a ten-piece order you should do considerably better**, and these companies quote custom for free.

Budget **$250–450 per rectangle** and **$150–300 per triangle** until you have real quotes. That's roughly **$2,000–3,750 for five dividers**, landing between the fence-framework build (~$1,490) and 80/20 (~$3,950) — but with zero fabrication labor on your end and a finish that already looks like a commercial product.

---

## The cheapest split

**Buy the rectangles stock, have the triangles made locally.**

The rectangle is 70% of the panel area and it's a commodity — no reason to pay custom rates for it. The triangle is small (93" × 24", about 8 sq ft) and five of them is a half-day job for a local shop working from angle iron and a scrap of matching mesh.

Local options already identified: [Vigil Steel](https://www.vigilsteel.com/) (Bigfork), [Flathead Welding Solutions](https://flatheadwelding.com/) (mobile, Kalispell), [Acutech Metalworks](https://acutechworks.com/) (Kalispell).

Order one rectangle first, take it to the welder, and have them match the frame and mesh on a triangle. Then you know exactly what five costs before committing.

## Why this may beat every other option in this repo

| Approach | 5 dividers | Fabrication labor | Finish |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stock partition panels** | **~$2,000–3,750** | **None** | Commercial, powder coated |
| Fence framework | ~$1,490 | High — you build it | Looks like a fence |
| [80/20](8020-BOM.md) | ~$3,950 | Medium — you assemble it | Clean, industrial |
| [Kee Klamp](keeklamp-BOM.md) | ~$5,525 | Medium | Galvanized, rated |

The partition-panel route is the only one where **somebody else already welded it**, which is the actual expensive part of every other option. It also arrives powder coated, which is a line item everywhere else.
